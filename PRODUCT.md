# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Overseas university and program admissions readers, plus teachers and students at international schools. They need to understand ChunYin Shang’s current academic direction, development, capabilities, and evidence of contribution within a short visit.

## Product Purpose

An English-language personal profile and writing archive that connects ChunYin’s questions, research, documentary work, leadership, public speaking, and essays. Success means a visitor forms an accurate first impression and can continue into detailed evidence or contact him directly.

## Positioning

The site connects life between Hangzhou and Hong Kong with sustained inquiry into PPE, STS, public policy, political science, and sociology. Real projects, public-speaking photographs, and first-person essays provide evidence rather than generic claims.

## Operating Context

A public React, TypeScript, and Vite site deployed through GitHub Pages at the project subpath `/chunyin-shang/`. Routing uses HashRouter and relative assets. Primary actions are exploring the profile, reading essays, filtering the archive, and email contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All public-facing content is English. Contact exposes only ChunYin Shang’s real name and email address. Preserve complete essays, working search and filters, reading progress, responsive behavior, accessible navigation, and factual project status. The documentary is in production: a 40-plus-minute interview with a Form 6 student at a Band 1 Hong Kong secondary school is complete, and episode one is being edited.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name ChunYin Shang (Khelil), the locations Hangzhou and Hong Kong, and Times New Roman throughout the site, including its favicon. The visual direction should feel editorial and academically credible without becoming generic or excessively formal.

## Evidence on Hand

Three complete essays, a comparative public-toilet research project, documentary status and subject matter, education and academic records, learning programs, student leadership, and two genuine public-speaking photographs in `public/images/`. Do not invent awards, outcomes, publications, interviews, or testimonials.

## Product Principles

Lead with inquiry and demonstrated work, not self-praise. Connect interface emphasis to real evidence. Keep long-form reading and archive navigation clear. Make the two-city perspective and current intellectual direction visible without reducing the person to a list of achievements.
