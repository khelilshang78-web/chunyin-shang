# Home surface brief

## Direction contract

THESIS: An admissions reader should meet a precise intellectual question, then see authentic evidence of inquiry, communication, and collaboration—not a colour concept competing with the person.
OWN-WORLD: Paper #fafafa, ink #0a0a0a, hairline #e5e5e5; natural-colour documentary photographs; Times New Roman throughout; sharp edges, generous intervals, no decorative accent.
STORY: Identity and academic direction → a large intellectual question → one panoramic moment of collaboration → current research and leadership → two contrasting experiences → selected essays → direct contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet horizontal navigation, a large but controlled two-line question, concise identity/location/field statement, and the beginning of the panoramic documentary photograph. No split colour, sidebar, or full-screen motion.
FORM: Reference-led monochrome editorial narrative; asymmetric image scale and hairline structure; code-first because the local design engine is unavailable.
FINISH: Verify real copy, all images, filters, long-form reading, keyboard states, and 320/375/390px mobile composition; independent finish review and current DESIGN.md before shipping.
